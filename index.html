2 — Future picture

Ode in three years.

Decisions based on models, not intuition alone. A production manager takes a feed strategy decision on a generation and utsett. The model has followed that generation from egg. Biological data from Mercatus, cost picture from D365, probable market price at slaughter time. The decision takes minutes, not days. The audit trail is complete.
Finance runs on real-time data. IAS 41 compatible biomass estimates produced continuously from live pen data. The auditor gets a data foundation, not a number.
The system knows before the person does. Deviations from plan surface immediately, routed to the right person with the right context.
The integrated team meets to find the next improvement, not to discuss system status. Ode owns the backlog. Vivicta pushes back. That dynamic continues across the full lifecycle.
3 — Our definition of ERP

F&O is the digital core.
ERP is the sum of three things.

01
Technology
D365 F&O as the digital core. Transactions, data production, integrations. The foundation must be solid.
02
Process design
How Ode organises its workflows. Who owns which decisions. Designed before configuration, not after.
03
Human interaction
How people actually behave in relation to the system. This is where value is either realised or lost.

Adoption is not a training problem. It is a governance problem. If business leaders do not own the value case for their own areas, no amount of training will close the gap.

4 — What AI-first actually requires

Infrastructure and working method.

Technical foundation
Data quality as a design principle from day one, not a remediation exercise.
Generation and utsett continuity from egg to slaughter. Mercatus integration without information loss.
AI initiatives fail on data foundations, not algorithms. That is the failure we are here to prevent.
Working method across the full lifecycle
Ode and Vivicta as one integrated team. Ode's subject matter experts in process design from the start, not as approvers at the end.
AI tools used actively in the delivery itself, building habits and competence while work is in progress.
An AI-first organisation builds through Lean improvement cycles after MVP. The capability grows over time, it does not arrive at go-live.
5 — Vivicta's approach to Ode

We steer toward Desired Outcomes, not milestones.

Outcomes as governance mechanism. Milestones measure activity. Desired Outcomes measure value. That distinction shapes how every decision is made throughout the engagement.
Process design before configuration. We do not open the system before we understand how Ode works and how Ode wants to work. Domain immersion is the prerequisite for accountability on outcomes.
MVP is the starting point. A working system covering core needs from day one, improved continuously through Lean cycles where Ode owns the backlog. The investment delivers value immediately and the system grows with the organisation.
Go-live is not the finish line. It is the beginning of the phase where the system proves its value.

7 — Cost model and partnership

Principle over rates.
Shared risk and upside.

Track 1
Cost plus margin, open book
You see what we pay, what margin we take, and why. No hidden markups. No surprises on the next invoice.
Track 2
Outcome bonus in tranches
Tied directly to Ode's Desired Outcomes. First tranche at documented baseline achievement. Second and third require delivery beyond that.

The outcome pot in Track 2 must always exceed what we would earn on standard time and materials alone. That is the anchor of the Vested model. If it does not hold, it is not a real Vested partnership.
